Data sources

What the dashboard shows

Open a parcel and select “View all recorded readings (JSON)” for its timestamps, provider labels, values, and available satellite comparison dates and bounds.

  • Open-Meteo weather supplies temperature, wind, rainfall and soil moisture at the parcel centroid. These are gridded estimates. Open-Meteo air quality supplies PM2.5.
  • NASA GIBS supplies Terra MODIS corrected-reflectance true-color frames. We compare frames seven days apart, including land around the parcel. Users can also supply their own aligned image pair.
  • USGS 3DEP supplies measured elevation for the 3D surface. OpenStreetMap contributors supply the basemap. The Caldwell demo uses Esri World Imagery for its aerial texture. Other parcels can use a user-uploaded site image.
  • Linked Ada County outlines come from the Ada County Highway District parcel map, maintained by the assessor. Parcelsite periodically checks the saved parcel number for changes.
  • Other parcel boundaries come from lease exhibit coordinates, uploaded GeoJSON or KML, or points drawn by the user. Users supply the lease text, confirmed rules, notes and inspection findings. View fictional sample parcels, exhibits and mesh files.

What the models learned from

  • Heat forest — WeatherBench 2 ERA5, six-hourly near-surface temperature from 50 US grid cells, 2015–2019. Exact dataset and split.
  • Soil and rain forest — Open-Meteo historical archive, ERA5-Land soil and temperature and ERA5 precipitation at six sites, 2019–2023. Site coordinates and split.
  • Satellite forest — historical NASA GIBS Terra MODIS image pairs at four regional sites, 2022–2024. Dates, sites and split.
  • Lease classifier — 25 team-authored examples across five categories. Read every labeled example.
  • Shap-E fine-tuning — Cap3D captions and latent representations of Objaverse objects. The final round used 32 training objects and seven held out. Browse captions, object links, creators and licenses.

Generated results

Nova Lite on Bedrock summarizes saved parcel records. FLUX and Shap-E on SageMaker use prompts to generate proposed features. Their outputs are illustrations. Alerts come from comparing recorded values with confirmed rules.
